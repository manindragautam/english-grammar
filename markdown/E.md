## 1: Eloquent
- **Dissection:** [elo-quent]
- **Meaning:** Fluently persuasive and articulate
- **Hindi:** सुवक्ता
- **Examples:**
  1. The president delivered an eloquent speech that inspired millions of citizens.
  2. His eloquent arguments persuaded the jury to reach a quick verdict.

## 2: Empathy
- **Dissection:** [em-pathy]
- **Meaning:** Identification with or understanding of the thoughts, feelings, or emotional state of another person.
- **Hindi:** समानुभूति
- **Examples:**
  1. She had a lot of empathy for her neighbor; she knew what it was like to lose a parent too.
  2. He felt deep empathy for the refugees who had lost their homes.

## 3: Enthusiastic
- **Dissection:** [en-thu-si-ast-ic]
- **Meaning:** With zealous fervor; excited, motivated.
- **Hindi:** उत्साही
- **Examples:**
  1. An enthusiastic lover of art
  2. She received an enthusiastic welcome from her new colleagues on her first day.

## 4: Essential
- **Dissection:** [es-sen-tial]
- **Meaning:** A necessary ingredient.
- **Hindi:** आवश्यक
- **Examples:**
  1. Don’t mind him being grumpy. that’s the essential fred.
  2. It is essential to double-check your calculations before submitting the report.
