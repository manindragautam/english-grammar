## 1: Eloquent
- **Dissection:** [elo-quent]
- **Meaning:** Fluent and persuasive in speaking or writing; articulate and expressive.
- **Hindi:** सुवक्ता
- **Examples:**
  1. The president delivered an eloquent speech that inspired millions of citizens to take action.
  2. Her eloquent arguments persuaded the jury to reach a more just verdict.
  3. He is known for his eloquent writing style, which makes even complex topics easy to understand.
  4. We were impressed by her eloquent defense of the new environmental policies.
  5. You need to be more eloquent during the interview to truly convey your skills and passion.

## 2: Empathy
- **Dissection:** [em-pathy]
- **Meaning:** The ability to understand and share the feelings, thoughts, or experiences of another person.
- **Hindi:** समानुभूति
- **Examples:**
  1. She showed great empathy for her neighbor who was struggling to cope with a recent loss.
  2. Having worked in social services, he felt deep empathy for the refugees seeking asylum.
  3. It is important for managers to show empathy toward their employees' personal challenges.
  4. His letters were filled with empathy and understanding for those facing similar hardships.
  5. Practicing active listening is a great way to build empathy in your personal relationships.

## 3: Enthusiastic
- **Dissection:** [en-thu-si-ast-ic]
- **Meaning:** Showing intense and eager enjoyment, interest, or approval; full of energy and excitement.
- **Hindi:** उत्साही
- **Examples:**
  1. An enthusiastic lover of art, she never misses an opening at the local gallery.
  2. She received an enthusiastic welcome from her new colleagues on her first day at the office.
  3. The team was enthusiastic about starting the new project and began brainstorming immediately.
  4. He gave an enthusiastic presentation that kept everyone engaged until the very end.
  5. We are looking for enthusiastic volunteers to help organize the community garden festival.

## 4: Essential
- **Dissection:** [es-sen-tial]
- **Meaning:** Absolutely necessary; extremely important; fundamental.
- **Hindi:** आवश्यक
- **Examples:**
  1. Proper hydration is essential when you're working in such high temperatures all day.
  2. It is essential to double-check your figures before submitting the final quarterly report.
  3. Good communication skills are essential for success in any leadership role.
  4. These key ingredients are essential for making a truly authentic pasta sauce.
  5. Patience is an essential quality for anyone who wants to become a great teacher.
