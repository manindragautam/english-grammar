## 1: Harmonious
- **Dissection:** [har-mo-ni-ous]
- **Meaning:** Showing accord in feeling or action.
- **Hindi:** सामंजस्यपूर्ण
- **Examples:**
  1. The team worked in a harmonious manner to complete the project ahead of schedule.
  2. The music was a harmonious blend of traditional and modern instruments.

## 2: Humble
- **Dissection:** [humble]
- **Meaning:** (Baltimore) An arrest based on weak evidence intended to demean or punish the subject.
- **Hindi:** विनम्र
- **Examples:**
  1. He lives in a humble one-bedroom cottage.
  2. She was very humble about her achievements, always sharing credit with her team.

## 3: Hesitant
- **Dissection:** [hes-it-ant]
- **Meaning:** Tending to hesitate, wait, or proceed with caution or reservation.
- **Hindi:** दुविधा में पड़ा हुआ
- **Examples:**
  1. I am hesitant to recommend him as a manager because he has a short temper.
  2. She gave a hesitant reply, unsure if she was ready for the responsibility.

## 4: Hilarious
- **Dissection:** [hil-ari-ous]
- **Meaning:** Very funny; causing great merriment and laughter.
- **Hindi:** आनंददायक
- **Examples:**
  1. A hilarious joke
  2. We spent the evening watching a hilarious comedy movie together.
