## 1: Majestic
- **Dissection:** [majest-ic]
- **Meaning:** Having qualities of splendor or royalty.
- **Hindi:** आलीशान
- **Examples:**
  1. The majestic snow-capped peaks of the Himalayas rose high into the blue sky.
  2. An eagle soaring gracefully above the canyon is a truly majestic sight.

## 2: Meticulous
- **Dissection:** [me-tic-u-lous]
- **Meaning:** Characterized by very precise, conscientious attention to details.
- **Hindi:** सूक्ष्म
- **Examples:**
  1. The researcher kept meticulous records of every step in the scientific experiment.
  2. The dressmaker paid meticulous attention to every single stitch of the gown.

## 3: Mindful
- **Dissection:** [mind-ful]
- **Meaning:** Being aware (of something); attentive, heedful.
- **Hindi:** सावधान
- **Examples:**
  1. We must be mindful of our environmental impact and reduce plastic waste.
  2. Being mindful of his health, he exercised daily and ate balanced meals.

## 4: Motivate
- **Dissection:** [mo-tiv-ate]
- **Meaning:** To provide someone with an incentive to do something; to encourage.
- **Hindi:** उत्साह करना
- **Examples:**
  1. The weekly staff meeting was meant to motivate employees.
  2. He was motivated purely by self-interest.
