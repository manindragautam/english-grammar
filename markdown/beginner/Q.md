## 1: Quality
- **Dissection:** [qual-ity]
- **Meaning:** An essential and distinguishing attribute of something or someone; --Shakespeare
- **Hindi:** गुणवत्ता
- **Examples:**
  1. The quality of mercy is not strained.
  2. The quality of students has risen.
