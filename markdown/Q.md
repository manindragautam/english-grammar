## 1: Quaint
- **Dissection:** [quaint]
- **Meaning:** Of a person: cunning, crafty.
- **Hindi:** विचित्र
- **Examples:**
  1. The tourists stayed in a quaint cottage with a thatched roof and a lovely garden.
  2. We walked down the cobblestone streets of a quaint medieval village.

## 2: Quintessential
- **Dissection:** [quint-es-sen-tial]
- **Meaning:** Of the nature of a quintessence (in all senses); ultimate.
- **Hindi:** सर्वोत्कृष्ट
- **Examples:**
  1. A red telephone box is a quintessential symbol of British culture.
  2. He is the quintessential gentleman, always polite, helpful, and well-mannered.

## 3: Quirk
- **Dissection:** [quirk]
- **Meaning:** An idiosyncrasy; a slight glitch, mannerism; something unusual about the manner or style of something or someone
- **Hindi:** मोड़
- **Examples:**
  1. The car steers cleanly, but the gearshift has a few quirks.
  2. He quirked an eyebrow.

## 4: Quench
- **Dissection:** [quench]
- **Meaning:** The abnormal termination of operation of a superconducting magnet, occurring when part of the superconducting coil enters the normal (resistive) state.
- **Hindi:** बुझाना
- **Examples:**
  1. The library quenched her thirst for knowledge.
  2. The swordsmith quenched the sword in an oil bath so that it wouldn't shatter.
