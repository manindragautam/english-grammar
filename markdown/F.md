## 1: Fascinate
- **Dissection:** [fas-cin-ate]
- **Meaning:** To evoke an intense interest or attraction in someone.
- **Hindi:** फ़ैसिनेट
- **Examples:**
  1. The flickering tv fascinated the cat.
  2. We were fascinated by the potter's skill.
