## 1: Fascinate
- **Dissection:** [fas-cin-ate]
- **Meaning:** To evoke an intense interest or attraction in someone; to charm or captivate.
- **Hindi:** रिझाना
- **Examples:**
  1. The way she describes her travels completely fascinates anyone who listens to her stories.
  2. We were fascinated by the intricate architecture of the ancient ruins during our trip.
  3. His ability to multitask in high-pressure situations never ceases to fascinate his colleagues.
  4. The mesmerizing performance of the acrobats fascinates children and adults alike.
  5. I am constantly fascinated by how quickly technology is evolving today.

## 2: Flawless
- **Dissection:** [flaw-less]
- **Meaning:** Without any imperfections, defects, or shortcomings; perfect in every way.
- **Hindi:** बेदाग
- **Examples:**
  1. The dancer's execution of the complex routine was absolutely flawless.
  2. Her presentation skills are spotless; she delivered a flawless pitch to the board today.
  3. They worked hard to ensure that the new website launch would be flawless and bug-free.
  4. His spoken English is nearly flawless, making him highly effective in international meetings.
  5. The diamond was described as a flawless gem with no visible inclusions or marks.

## 3: Formidable
- **Dissection:** [for-mid-able]
- **Meaning:** Inspiring fear, dread, or awe due to size, strength, power, or some other impressive quality; commanding respect or posing a significant challenge.
- **Hindi:** दुर्जेय
- **Examples:**
  1. The defending team was a formidable opponent that struggled against the champions.
  2. Climbing the peak of K2 is a formidable task that requires years of training and preparation.
  3. She faced a formidable challenge when she had to lead the company through a major merger.
  4. Despite its size, the small startup proved to be a formidable competitor in the tech market.
  5. The scholar became known for her formidable intellect and deep knowledge of history.

## 4: Frugal
- **Dissection:** [frugal]
- **Meaning:** Economical and careful with money or resources; avoiding waste and unnecessary spending.
- **Hindi:** मितव्ययी
- **Examples:**
  1. By living a frugal lifestyle, she managed to save enough for her retirement in just a few years.
  2. He is very frugal when it comes to groceries, always looking for deals and buying in bulk.
  3. They prepared a frugal but delicious meal using leftovers from the previous night's dinner.
  4. Being mindful of our resources is essential; we should aim to be more frugal with water and energy.
  5. He was known for his frugal habits, often repairing old furniture instead of buying new pieces.
