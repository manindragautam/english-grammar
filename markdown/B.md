## 1: Bizarre
- **Dissection:** [bizarre]
- **Meaning:** Strangely unconventional in style or appearance.
- **Hindi:** विचित्र
- **Examples:**
  1. The detective investigated a bizarre case that seemed to defy all logical explanation.
  2. We experienced some bizarre weather conditions, including snow in the middle of summer.

## 2: Benevolent
- **Dissection:** [be-ne-vol-ent]
- **Meaning:** Having a disposition to do good.
- **Hindi:** परोपकारी
- **Examples:**
  1. Chinese and eastern mythologies describe dragons as benevolent.
  2. She was known throughout the town for her benevolent nature and charity work.

## 4: Bewilder
- **Dissection:** [be-wilder]
- **Meaning:** To confuse, disorientate, or puzzle someone, especially with many different choices.
- **Hindi:** भुलाना
- **Examples:**
  1. All the different possible options may bewilder us.
  2. The sudden changes in the project requirements bewildered the development team.
