## 1: Bizarre
- **Dissection:** [bizarre]
- **Meaning:** Very unusual, strange, or unexpected in a way that is slightly disturbing.
- **Hindi:** विचित्र
- **Examples:**
  1. The office layout was so bizarre that I kept bumping into walls every few minutes.
  2. It’s bizarre how they managed to finish the entire project without any meetings.
  3. I heard a bizarre noise coming from the basement last night, but when I checked, nothing was there.
  4. Using AI to write poetry is a bizarre but fascinating development in modern technology.
  5. There's something bizarre about how quickly everyone moved on after the scandal occurred.



## 2: Benevolent
- **Dissection:** [be-ne-vol-ent]
- **Meaning:** Kind and generous; showing a desire to do good for others and help those in need.
- **Hindi:** परोपकारी
- **Examples:**
  1. The local community center receives many benevolent donations from nearby businesses.
  2. She is known for her benevolent nature, always looking for ways to help the homeless.
  3. The billionaire's benevolent foundation provides free healthcare in rural areas.
  4. He made a benevolent gesture by paying for the meal of the family at the next table.
  5. We are seeking a benevolent investor who cares about social impact as much as profit.



## 3: Breakthrough
- **Dissection:** [break-through]
- **Meaning:** A significant and important discovery, development, or achievement in a specific area.
- **Hindi:** सफलता / महत्वपूर्ण उपलब्धि
- **Examples:**
  1. The team finally achieved a breakthrough in their research on finding a cure for the disease.
  2. Smart glasses represent a major technological breakthrough for augmented reality enthusiasts.
  3. After months of debugging, they had a breakthrough and fixed the core architectural issue.
  4. Her exploration led to a significant archaeological breakthrough about early human settlers.
  5. The discovery of efficient solid-state batteries is a huge breakthrough in energy storage.



## 4: Bewilder
- **Dissection:** [be-wilder]
- **Meaning:** To cause someone to become extremely confused or perplexed; to baffle.
- **Hindi:** चकित करना / उलझन में डालना
- **Examples:**
  1. The complex tax laws can often bewilder even the most experienced accountants.
  2. It was bewildering how many thousands of people could gather in such a small space.
  3. The magician's use of mirrors continued to bewilder the children.
  4. I'm bewildered by the sudden change in leadership at our tech company.
  5. Her bizarre behavior began to bewilder her colleagues, who didn't know how to react.
