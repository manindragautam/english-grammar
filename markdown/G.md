## 1: Genuine
- **Dissection:** [genu-ine]
- **Meaning:** Real; authentic; proceeding from true origins rather than being artificial or counterfeit.
- **Hindi:** असली
- **Examples:**
  1. Despite the scandal, she proved to have a genuine concern for the welfare of her employees.
  2. I'm looking for a genuine leather jacket that will last for many years.
  3. He showed a genuine interest in learning about different cultures and traditions during his travels.
  4. It was a genuine surprise to find out they had organized such a beautiful celebration for us.
  5. Their friendship is based on a genuine connection rather than professional convenience.

## 2: Grateful
- **Dissection:** [grate-ful]
- **Meaning:** Feeling or showing appreciation and thanks towards someone for their kindness, help, or service.
- **Hindi:** आभारी
- **Examples:**
  1. I am so grateful for all the support my family has given me during this difficult time.
  2. We are deeply grateful to the volunteers who worked tirelessly to organize the charity event.
  3. She felt incredibly grateful that she was offered the job after such a long and tiring interview process.
  4. I'm grateful that you helped me out when things got really complicated last week.
  5. They expressed their gratitude by sending a handwritten thank-you note to everyone involved.

## 3: Gregarious
- **Dissection:** [gregari-ous]
- **Meaning:** (Of a person) Fond of company; sociable; enjoying being in a group or socializing with others.
- **Hindi:** झुण्ड में रहनेवाला
- **Examples:**
  1. Being naturally gregarious, he made friends easily at his new workplace and enjoyed the social lunch break.
  2. Dolphins are known to be Gregarious animals that live and hunt in highly organized social groups.
  3. She is very gregarious and always organizes get-togethers for our neighborhood residents.
  4. His gregarious personality makes him an excellent host for large parties and events.
  5. While I'm a bit of an introvert, my coworker is extremely gregarious and loves meeting new people.

## 4: Galvanize
- **Dissection:** [gal-van-ize]
- **Meaning:** To shock or excite someone into taking sudden action; to mobilizeing into active behavior.
- **Hindi:** सक्रिय करना (Note: The previous 'कलई' is a literal metal coating, which was incorrect in context)
- **Examples:**
  1. The tragic news served to galvanize the community into demanding immediate changes to safety laws.
  2. The manager’s passionate speech was intended to galvanize the team before the final deadline.
  3. We need a clear set of goals to galvanize our efforts toward achieving complete project success.
  4. The company's new advertising campaign is designed to galvanize interest in their sustainable products.
  5. They hoped that the donation drive would galvanize support for the local animal shelter.
