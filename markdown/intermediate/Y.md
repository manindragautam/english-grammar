## 1: Yacht
- **Dissection:** [यॉट] (yacht - एक बार में बोलें)
- **Meaning:** A boat with sails and sometimes an engine, used for either racing or traveling on for pleasure.
- **Hindi:** नौका / छोटी नाव
- **Examples:**
  1. The rich famous actor bought a very large expensive white **yacht**.
  2. We sailed the beautiful wooden **yacht** across the deep blue sea.

## 2: Yarn
- **Dissection:** [यार्न] (yarn - एक बार में बोलें)
- **Meaning:** Thread used for making cloth or for knitting.
- **Hindi:** धागा (ऊन या सूत)
- **Examples:**
  1. She completely knitted a very warm sweater with soft red wool **yarn**.
  2. The playful little cat completely tangled the ball of blue **yarn**.

## 3: Yawn
- **Dissection:** [यॉन] (yawn - एक बार में बोलें)
- **Meaning:** To open the mouth wide and take a lot of air into the lungs and slowly send it out, usually when tired or bored.
- **Hindi:** जम्हाई लेना
- **Examples:**
  1. The very tired young baby began to quietly **yawn** and rub his little eyes.
  2. He tried very hard not to loudly **yawn** during the long boring meeting.

## 4: Yield
- **Dissection:** [यील्ड] (yield - एक बार में बोलें)
- **Meaning:** To supply or produce something positive such as a profit, an amount of food or information; or to give up the control of or responsibility for something, often because you have been forced to.
- **Hindi:** उपज / हार मानना / पैदावार
- **Examples:**
  1. The very good dark soil will surely **yield** a healthy completely large crop.
  2. The stubborn completely angry general refused to ever properly **yield** to the strong enemy.

## 5: Yolk
- **Dissection:** [योक] (yolk - एक बार में बोलें)
- **Meaning:** The yellow, middle part of an egg.
- **Hindi:** जर्दी (अंडे का पीला भाग)
- **Examples:**
  1. I completely prefer to slowly eat the soft yellow **yolk** of the fresh egg.
  2. She completely carefully separated the bright orange **yolk** from the clear white.
