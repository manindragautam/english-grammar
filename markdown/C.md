## 1: Candid
- **Dissection:** [can-did]
- **Meaning:** A spontaneous or unposed photograph.
- **Hindi:** स्पष्टवादी
- **Examples:**
  1. His portraits looked stiff and formal but his candids showed life being lived.
  2. We took a few candid photographs of the children playing in the garden.

## 2: Compassion
- **Dissection:** [com-pas-sion]
- **Meaning:** Deep awareness of the suffering of another, coupled with the wish to relieve it.
- **Hindi:** करुणा
- **Examples:**
  1. The nurse showed great compassion toward the suffering patients under her care.
  2. Driven by compassion, the community raised funds to help the family rebuild their house.

## 3: Captivate
- **Dissection:** [cap-tiv-ate]
- **Meaning:** To attract and hold interest and attention of; charm.
- **Hindi:** मैं एक नोबेल पढ़ रहा हूँ
- **Examples:**
  1. The magician's incredible illusions managed to captivate the entire audience.
  2. Her beautiful voice and emotional performance captivated the music judges.

## 4: Crucial
- **Dissection:** [cru-cial]
- **Meaning:** Essential or decisive for determining the outcome or future of something; extremely important.
- **Hindi:** महत्वपूर्ण
- **Examples:**
  1. A secure supply of crude oil is crucial for any modern nation, let alone a superpower.
  2. Delbert wilkins is the most crucial pirate radio dj in brixton.
