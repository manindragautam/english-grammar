## 1: Candid
- **Dissection:** [can-did]
- **Meaning:** Truthful and straightforward; honest and sincere in expression.
- **Hindi:** स्पष्टवादी
- **Examples:**
  1. I appreciate your candid feedback on my presentation; it helped me see the flaws clearly.
  2. She gave a candid interview about her struggles with mental health.
  3. We had a candid conversation about the budget, and everyone was honest about their concerns.
  4. He is known for his candid remarks during town hall meetings.
  5. I need you to be completely candid with me about what went wrong during the project.

## 2: Compassion
- **Dissection:** [com-pas-sion]
- **Meaning:** A feeling of deep sympathy and concern for others who are suffering or in misfortune.
- **Hindi:** करुणा
- **Examples:**
  1. The wildlife sanctuary was built out of compassion for the endangered species in the area.
  2. We were moved by her deep compassion for the refugees seeking asylum.
  3. He showed great compassion by spending his weekends volunteering at the local hospice.
  4. It takes a lot of courage and compassion to forgive someone who has deeply hurt you.
  5. Her immense compassion for the homeless population led her to start a soup kitchen.

## 3: Captivate
- **Dissection:** [cap-tiv-ate]
- **Meaning:** To attract and hold the attention or interest of; to charm or fascinate.
- **Hindi:** आकर्षित करना
- **Examples:**
  1. The mesmerizing performance of the street dancers managed to captivate everyone who passed by.
  2. Her ability to weave stories was enough to captivate the most disinterested audience.
  3. I found myself captivated by the intricate details of the old watch's movement.
  4. The vibrant colors and alien landscapes of the movie captivated millions worldwide.
  5. The stunning sunrise over the mountains never fails to captivate me every morning.

## 4: Crucial
- **Dissection:** [cru-cial]
- **Meaning:** Extremely important or necessary; of great significance or importance.
- **Hindi:** महत्वपूर्ण
- **Examples:**
  1. Timing is crucial when you're trying to coordinate a meeting with everyone in different time zones.
  2. A sturdy foundation is crucial for the structural integrity of any building.
  3. Your participation in the feedback session is crucial for improving our new software.
  4. We need to make some crucial decisions regarding the company's future expansion by next week.
  5. It is crucial that we maintain clear communication during this transition period.
