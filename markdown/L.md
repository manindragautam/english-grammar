## 1: Lucid
- **Dissection:** [lu-cid]
- **Meaning:** A lucid dream.
- **Hindi:** स्पष्ट अर्थ का
- **Examples:**
  1. The teacher gave a lucid explanation of a very complex mathematical formula.
  2. Despite his advanced age, he remained lucid and remembered historical events clearly.

## 2: Lucrative
- **Dissection:** [luc-rat-ive]
- **Meaning:** Producing a surplus; profitable.
- **Hindi:** लाभप्रद
- **Examples:**
  1. Investing in real estate turned out to be a highly lucrative venture for them.
  2. She left her teaching job to pursue a more lucrative career in software engineering.

## 3: Luminous
- **Dissection:** [lu-min-ous]
- **Meaning:** Emitting light; glowing brightly.
- **Hindi:** प्रकाशमान
- **Examples:**
  1. The watch hands were coated with luminous paint so they could be read in the dark.
  2. The night sky was filled with luminous stars reflecting on the calm lake surface.

## 4: Logical
- **Dissection:** [lo-gic-al]
- **Meaning:** In agreement with the principles of logic.
- **Hindi:** तार्किक
- **Examples:**
  1. Logical memory appears contiguous to an application program, but may well be stored on several physical devices, including in ram and on hard-disks, as determined by the operating system.
  2. He presented a logical argument that was easy for everyone to follow.
