## 1: Ubiquitous
- **Dissection:** [ubi-quit-ous]
- **Meaning:** Being everywhere at once: omnipresent.
- **Hindi:** सर्वव्यापी
- **Examples:**
  1. To hindus, jews, christians, and muslims, god is ubiquitous.
  2. The use of ubiquitous made the meaning very clear.

## 2: Unique
- **Dissection:** [unique]
- **Meaning:** A thing without a like; something unequalled or unparallelled.
- **Hindi:** बेजोड़
- **Examples:**
  1. Every person has a unique life, therefore every person has a unique journey. ― gary cook
  2. The use of unique made the meaning very clear.

## 3: Unanimous
- **Dissection:** [un-an-im-ous]
- **Meaning:** Based on unanimity, assent or agreement.
- **Hindi:** Unanimous का हिंदी अर्थ
- **Examples:**
  1. The debate went on for hours, but in the end the decision was unanimous.
  2. We were unanimous: the president had to go.

## 4: Uplifting
- **Dissection:** [up-lift-ing]
- **Meaning:** The act of something being lifted upward.
- **Hindi:** उत्थान
- **Examples:**
  1. Listening to whalesong can be very uplifting.
  2. The use of uplifting made the meaning very clear.
