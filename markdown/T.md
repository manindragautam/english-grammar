## 1: Tenacious
- **Dissection:** [ten-a-cious]
- **Meaning:** Clinging to an object or surface; adhesive.
- **Hindi:** दृढ़
- **Examples:**
  1. With a tenacious grip, the climber refused to let go of the rocky ledge.
  2. Her tenacious defense of human rights won her international recognition.

## 2: Tranquil
- **Dissection:** [tran-quil]
- **Meaning:** Free from emotional or mental disturbance.
- **Hindi:** शांत
- **Examples:**
  1. The house overlooked a tranquil lake surrounded by quiet pine forests.
  2. She meditated every morning to maintain a tranquil state of mind throughout the day.

## 3: Thrive
- **Dissection:** [thrive]
- **Meaning:** To grow or increase stature; to grow vigorously or luxuriantly, to flourish.
- **Hindi:** फलना-फूलना
- **Examples:**
  1. Not all animals thrive well in captivity.
  2. Since expanding in june, the business has really thrived.

## 4: Tangible
- **Dissection:** [tan-gible]
- **Meaning:** A physical object, something that can be touched.
- **Hindi:** वास्तविक
- **Examples:**
  1. Yes, but what are the tangibles?
  2. There was a tangible sense of excitement in the air before the concert started.
