## 1: Pragmatic
- **Dissection:** [prag-mat-ic]
- **Meaning:** A man of business.
- **Hindi:** व्यावहारिक
- **Examples:**
  1. The sturdy furniture in the student lounge was pragmatic, but unattractive.
  2. She is a pragmatic decision-maker who focuses on practical results.

## 2: Profound
- **Dissection:** [pro-found]
- **Meaning:** The deep; the sea; the ocean.
- **Hindi:** गहरा
- **Examples:**
  1. A profound investigation
  2. The sudden loss of his mentor left him in a state of profound sadness.

## 3: Persuade
- **Dissection:** [per-suade]
- **Meaning:** To successfully convince (someone) to agree to, accept, or do something, usually through reasoning and verbal influence.
- **Hindi:** राज़ी करना
- **Examples:**
  1. That salesman was able to persuade me into buying this bottle of lotion.
  2. Advertisements try to persuade consumers to buy products they may not need.

## 4: Pivotal
- **Dissection:** [pivotal]
- **Meaning:** Of, relating to, or being a pivot.
- **Hindi:** केंद्रीय
- **Examples:**
  1. The signing of the treaty was a pivotal moment in the history of the two nations.
  2. Her mentorship played a pivotal role in the success of my academic career.
