## 1: Zany
- **Dissection:** [zany]
- **Meaning:** A buffoon in one of the old comedies; imitates others for ludicrous effect
- **Hindi:** बौड़म
- **Examples:**
  1. Gave me a cockamamie reason for not going.
  2. Wore a goofy hat.

## 2: Zeal
- **Dissection:** [zeal]
- **Meaning:** A feeling of strong eagerness (usually in favor of a person or cause)
- **Hindi:** उत्साह
- **Examples:**
  1. They were imbued with a revolutionary ardor.
  2. He felt a kind of religious zeal.

## 3: Zealot
- **Dissection:** [zealot]
- **Meaning:** A member of an ancient Jewish sect in Judea in the first century who fought to the death against the Romans and who killed or persecuted Jews who collaborated with the Romans
- **Hindi:** कट्टरपंथी
- **Examples:**
  1. The religious zealot refused to tolerate any beliefs other than his own.
  2. She was an environmental zealot who spent all her time campaigning for reforms.

## 4: Zenith
- **Dissection:** [zenith]
- **Meaning:** The point in the sky vertically above a given position or observer; the point in the celestial sphere opposite the nadir.
- **Hindi:** शीर्षबिंदु
- **Examples:**
  1. Winning the continental championship was the zenith of my career.
  2. The sun reached its zenith in the sky, casting short shadows on the ground.

## 5: Zephyr
- **Dissection:** [zephyr]
- **Meaning:** A slight wind (usually refreshing)
- **Hindi:** हलकी हवा
- **Examples:**
  1. The breeze was cooled by the lake.
  2. As he waited he could feel the air on his neck.
