## 1: Xenophobia
- **Dissection:** [xeno-pho-bia]
- **Meaning:** A fear of foreigners or strangers
- **Hindi:** विदेशी लोगों को न पसन्द करना
- **Examples:**
  1. Or maybe it's sheer xenophobia.
  2. Xenophobia often rises during economic hardships when immigrants are unfairly blamed for a lack of jobs or resources.
