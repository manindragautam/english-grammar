## 1: Articulate
- **Dissection:** [ar-tic-u-late]
- **Meaning:** An animal of the subkingdom Articulata.
- **Hindi:** स्पष्ट करना
- **Examples:**
  1. She’s a bright, articulate young woman.
  2. The robot arm was articulate in two directions.

## 2: Authentic
- **Dissection:** [au-then-t-ic]
- **Meaning:** Of the same origin as claimed; genuine.
- **Hindi:** प्रामाणिक
- **Examples:**
  1. The experts confirmed it was an authentic signature.
  2. The report was completely authentic.

## 3: Astonish
- **Dissection:** [as-ton-ish]
- **Meaning:** To surprise greatly.
- **Hindi:** चकित
- **Examples:**
  1. The magician's ability to levitate objects astonished everyone in the room.
  2. It astonished me that they managed to finish the massive project in just a weekend.

## 4: Ambitious
- **Dissection:** [am-bi-tious]
- **Meaning:** (of a person or their character) Having or showing ambition; wanting a lot of power, honor, respect, superiority, or other distinction.
- **Hindi:** महत्वाकांक्षी
- **Examples:**
  1. An ambitious person
  2. An ambitious attempt to take power
