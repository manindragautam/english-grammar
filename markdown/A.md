## 1: Articulate
- **Dissection:** [ar-tic-u-late]
- **Meaning:** Having or showing the ability to speak fluently and coherently.
- **Hindi:** स्पष्ट करना
- **Examples:**
  1. The diplomat was noted for his ability to remain articulate even under intense scrutiny, ensuring that every point of his argument was communicated with perfect clarity.
  2. Exploring complex scientific theories requires an individual to be exceptionally articulate about abstract concepts in a way that is accessible to the general public.
  3. Her speech was so articulate and well-structured that it convinced the board members to invest in the new initiative immediately.

## 2: Authentic
- **Dissection:** [au-then-t-ic]
- **Meaning:** Of the same origin as claimed; genuine.
- **Hindi:** प्रामाणिक
- **Examples:**
  1. The museum displayed authentic artifacts from the Ming Dynasty, drawing huge crowds of history enthusiasts and scholars alike.
  2. It is difficult to find truly authentic experiences in a world increasingly dominated by mass tourism and fabricated aesthetics.
  3. Although she claimed to be an expert, her lack of knowledge revealed that her credentials were far from authentic.

## 3: Astonish
- **Dissection:** [as-ton-ish]
- **Meaning:** To surprise greatly.
- **Hindi:** चकित
- **Examples:**
  1. The sheer scale of the architectural feat would astonish anyone who had only ever seen traditional building methods.
  2. It is truly astonishing how quickly technology can evolve, often making yesterday's innovations look obsolete overnight.
  3. His ability to play three instruments simultaneously managed to astonish even the most seasoned music critics.

## 4: Ambitious
- **Dissection:** [am-bi-tious]
- **Meaning:** (of a person or their character) Having or showing ambition; wanting a lot of power, honor, respect, superiority, or other distinction.
- **Hindi:** महत्वाकांक्षी
- **Examples:**
  1. Setting up a global non-profit in under a year is an ambitious goal that requires significant resources and determination.
  2. The project's timeline was criticized as overly ambitious given the current limitations of the team's technology.
  3. She made an ambitious move by resigning from her stable position to pursue a dream of opening her own laboratory.
