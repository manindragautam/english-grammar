## 1: Keen
- **Dissection:** [keen]
- **Meaning:** To make cold, to sharpen.
- **Hindi:** उत्सुक
- **Examples:**
  1. I’m keen on computers.
  2. This boy has a keen appetite.

## 2: Knowledgeable
- **Dissection:** [know-ledge-able]
- **Meaning:** A person who has knowledge; an informed party.
- **Hindi:** जानकार
- **Examples:**
  1. The museum guide was highly knowledgeable about the history of the ancient ruins.
  2. If you want to start a business, consult with someone knowledgeable in the field.

## 3: Kinship
- **Dissection:** [kin-ship]
- **Meaning:** Relation or connection by blood, marriage or adoption
- **Hindi:** समानता
- **Examples:**
  1. Spending time together in the wilderness fostered a deep sense of kinship among the group.
  2. She felt a strong kinship with fellow artists who shared her creative vision.

## 4: Knack
- **Dissection:** [knack]
- **Meaning:** A readiness in performance; aptness at doing something.
- **Hindi:** आदत
- **Examples:**
  1. He has a natural knack for languages and learned to speak Spanish in just three months.
  2. Fixing broken clocks was a unique knack that he inherited from his grandfather.
