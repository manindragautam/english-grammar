## 1: Fascinate
- **Dissection:** [fas-cin-ate]
- **Meaning:** To evoke an intense interest or attraction in someone.
- **Hindi:** रिझाना
- **Examples:**
  1. The flickering tv fascinated the cat.
  2. We were fascinated by the potter's skill.

## 2: Flawless
- **Dissection:** [flaw-less]
- **Meaning:** Without flaws, defects, or shortcomings; perfect.
- **Hindi:** बेदाग
- **Examples:**
  1. The pianist’s performance this evening was flawless.
  2. Her spoken English is flawless and sounds just like a native speaker.

## 3: Formidable
- **Dissection:** [for-mid-able]
- **Meaning:** Causing fear, dread, awe, or discouragement as a result of size, strength, or some other impressive feature; commanding respect; causing wonder or astonishment.
- **Hindi:** दुर्जेय
- **Examples:**
  1. A formidable opponent
  2. The mountain range presented a formidable barrier to the early explorers.

## 4: Frugal
- **Dissection:** [frugal]
- **Meaning:** Avoiding unnecessary expenditure either of money or of anything else which is to be used or consumed; avoiding waste.
- **Hindi:** मितव्ययी
- **Examples:**
  1. By living a frugal lifestyle, she was able to save enough money to buy a house.
  2. He prepared a frugal but delicious meal using simple ingredients from his pantry.
