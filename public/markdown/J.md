## 1: Jovial
- **Dissection:** [jovi-al]
- **Meaning:** Cheerful and good-humoured; jolly, merry.
- **Hindi:** उल्लासपूर्ण
- **Examples:**
  1. The festive atmosphere put everyone in a jovial and celebratory mood.
  2. Our host was a jovial man who welcomed us with a warm smile and hearty laugh.

## 2: Justify
- **Dissection:** [jus-ti-fy]
- **Meaning:** To provide an acceptable explanation for.
- **Hindi:** औचित्य
- **Examples:**
  1. How can you justify spending so much money on clothes?
  2. Nothing can justify your rude behaviour last night.

## 3: Jubilant
- **Dissection:** [ju-bil-ant]
- **Meaning:** In a state of elation.
- **Hindi:** उल्लसित
- **Examples:**
  1. The crowd was jubilant when the home team scored the winning goal in the final second.
  2. Researchers were jubilant after achieving a major breakthrough in their cancer study.

## 4: Judicious
- **Dissection:** [ju-di-cious]
- **Meaning:** Having, characterized by, or done with good judgment or sound thinking.
- **Hindi:** उचित
- **Examples:**
  1. A judicious use of resources helped the startup survive its first critical year.
  2. The judge's judicious decision was praised by legal experts from both sides.
