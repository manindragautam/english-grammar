## 1: Xenophobia
- **Dissection:** [xeno-pho-bia]
- **Meaning:** A fear of foreigners or strangers
- **Hindi:** विदेशी लोगों को न पसन्द करना
- **Examples:**
  1. Learning the word 'xenophobia' can help improve your communication and writing skills.
  2. It is beneficial to understand the meaning and context of 'xenophobia' in daily conversations.
