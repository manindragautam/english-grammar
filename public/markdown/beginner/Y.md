## 1: Yard
- **Dissection:** [yard]
- **Meaning:** A unit of length equal to 3 feet; defined as 91.44 centimeters; originally taken to be the average length of a stride
- **Hindi:** यार्ड
- **Examples:**
  1. It was a small house with almost no yard.
  2. They opened a repair yard on the edge of town.

## 2: Yeah
- **Dissection:** [yeah]
- **Meaning:** Not only so, but
- **Hindi:** हाँ
- **Examples:**
  1. I therein do rejoice, yea, and will rejoice.
  2. Yeah! We did it!

## 3: Year
- **Dissection:** [year]
- **Meaning:** A period of time containing 365 (or 366) days
- **Hindi:** वर्ष
- **Examples:**
  1. She is 4 years old.
  2. In the year 1920.

## 4: Yellow

- **Hindi:** पीला
- **Examples:**

## 8: You
