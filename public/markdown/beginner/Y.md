## 1: Yard
- **Dissection:** [yard]
- **Meaning:** The outdoor area around a house, often covered with grass.
- **Hindi:** आँगन / यार्ड
- **Examples:**
  1. The kids are playing with their dog in the back yard.
  2. We need to mow the grass in the front yard this weekend.

## 2: Yeah
- **Dissection:** [yeah]
- **Meaning:** A casual way of saying "yes".
- **Hindi:** हाँ
- **Examples:**
  1. Yeah, I think I'll go to the movies tonight.
  2. "Are you hungry?" "Yeah, let's grab some pizza."

## 3: Year
- **Dissection:** [year]
- **Meaning:** A period of 365 days, divided into 12 months.
- **Hindi:** वर्ष / साल
- **Examples:**
  1. She is planning to travel to Japan next year.
  2. We have lived in this city for over a year now.

## 4: Yellow
- **Dissection:** [yel-low]
- **Meaning:** A bright color like that of a lemon or the sun.
- **Hindi:** पीला
- **Examples:**
  1. He wore a bright yellow shirt to the party.
  2. The leaves turn beautiful shades of red and yellow in autumn.

## 5: Yes
- **Dissection:** [yes]
- **Meaning:** Used to give an affirmative answer or agree to something.
- **Hindi:** हाँ
- **Examples:**
  1. Please answer yes or no to the following question.
  2. "Do you want some coffee?" "Yes, please."

## 6: Yesterday
- **Dissection:** [yes-ter-day]
- **Meaning:** The day immediately before today.
- **Hindi:** बीता हुआ कल
- **Examples:**
  1. I went to the grocery store yesterday afternoon.
  2. She was very tired yesterday because she didn't sleep well.

## 7: Yet
- **Dissection:** [yet]
- **Meaning:** Up until the present time or a specified time.
- **Hindi:** अभी तक
- **Examples:**
  1. Have you finished your homework yet?
  2. I am not ready to leave for the airport yet.

## 8: You
- **Dissection:** [you]
- **Meaning:** Used to refer to the person or people being addressed.
- **Hindi:** तुम / आप
- **Examples:**
  1. Are you going to the party tonight?
  2. I think you should wear a coat because it's cold outside.

## 9: Young
- **Dissection:** [young]
- **Meaning:** Having lived or existed for only a short time; not old.
- **Hindi:** युवा / जवान
- **Examples:**
  1. The young kitten was very playful and energetic.
  2. He started learning to play the piano at a very young age.

## 10: Your
- **Dissection:** [your]
- **Meaning:** Belonging to or associated with the person or people that the speaker is addressing.
- **Hindi:** तुम्हारा / आपका
- **Examples:**
  1. Don't forget to take your umbrella when you leave.
  2. I really like your new haircut.

## 11: Yourself
- **Dissection:** [your-self]
- **Meaning:** Used to refer to the person being addressed as the object of a verb or preposition when they are also the subject.
- **Hindi:** स्वयं / अपने आप को
- **Examples:**
  1. You need to believe in yourself if you want to succeed.
  2. Did you make this delicious cake all by yourself?

## 12: Youth
- **Dissection:** [youth]
- **Meaning:** The period of life when someone is young.
- **Hindi:** यौवन / युवावस्था
- **Examples:**
  1. He spent much of his youth traveling around Europe.
  2. The youth in our community need more after-school programs.
