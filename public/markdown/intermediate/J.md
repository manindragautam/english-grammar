## 1: Jail
- **Dissection:** [जेल] (jail - एक बार में बोलें)
- **Meaning:** A place where criminals are kept as a punishment.
- **Hindi:** जेल / कारागार
- **Examples:**
  1. The thief was sent to **jail** for stealing the money.
  2. He spent two years in **jail** before he was freed.

- **Examples:**
