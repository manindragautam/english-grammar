## 1: Xenial
- **Dissection:** [xeni-al]
- **Meaning:** Fond of or hospitable to strangers.
- **Hindi:** मिलनसार
- **Examples:**
  1. The hosts welcomed the weary travelers with warm and xenial hospitality.
  2. Their xenial attitude made the international students feel at home immediately.

## 2: Xenobiotic
- **Dissection:** [xen-o-bi-ot-ic]
- **Meaning:** Any foreign compound not produced by an organism's metabolism.
- **Hindi:** जीनोबायोटिक
- **Examples:**
  1. The laboratory tested how the liver metabolizes various xenobiotic substances.
  2. Marine biologists are studying the long-term effects of xenobiotic pollutants on fish.
