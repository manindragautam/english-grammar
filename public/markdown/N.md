## 1: Nuance
- **Dissection:** [nu-ance]
- **Meaning:** A minor distinction.
- **Hindi:** अति सूक्ष्म अंतर
- **Examples:**
  1. Understanding the basics is easy, but appreciating the nuances takes years.
  2. The actor's performance captured every nuance of the character's complex emotions.

## 2: Nostalgic
- **Dissection:** [nos-tal-gic]
- **Meaning:** A person who displays nostalgia for something.
- **Hindi:** बीते वक्त की याद
- **Examples:**
  1. Hearing the old song made him feel nostalgic about his high school days.
  2. The antique shop was filled with items that evoked a nostalgic feeling.

## 3: Notable
- **Dissection:** [not-able]
- **Meaning:** A person or thing of distinction.
- **Hindi:** प्रसिद्ध
- **Examples:**
  1. There has been a notable improvement in his grades since he started studying daily.
  2. The museum displays several notable paintings from the Renaissance era.
