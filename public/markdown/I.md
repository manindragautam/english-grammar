## 1: Intuitive
- **Dissection:** [in-tu-it-ive]
- **Meaning:** One who has (especially parapsychological) intuition.
- **Hindi:** सहज ज्ञान युक्त
- **Examples:**
  1. The intuitive response turned out to be correct.
  2. Designing software with an intuitive interface can be difficult.

## 2: Inevitable
- **Dissection:** [in-ev-it-able]
- **Meaning:** Something that is predictable, necessary, or cannot be avoided.
- **Hindi:** अपरिहार्य
- **Examples:**
  1. We were going so fast that the collision was inevitable.
  2. My outburst met with the inevitable punishment.

## 3: Inspiring
- **Dissection:** [in-spir-ing]
- **Meaning:** To infuse into the mind; to communicate to the spirit; to convey, as by a divine or supernatural influence; to disclose preternaturally; to produce in, as by inspiration.
- **Hindi:** प्रेरणादायक
- **Examples:**
  1. Elders should inspire children with sentiments of virtue.
  2. The use of inspiring made the meaning very clear.

## 4: Impeccable
- **Dissection:** [im-pec-cable]
- **Meaning:** Perfect, without faults, flaws or errors
- **Hindi:** त्रुटिहीन
- **Examples:**
  1. He grew up in norway, but he writes impeccable english.
  2. The use of impeccable made the meaning very clear.
