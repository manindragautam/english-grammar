- **Dissection:** [zenith]

- **Hindi:** शिरोबिन्दु
- **Examples:**

## 3: Zest
- **Dissection:** [zest]
- **Meaning:** The outer skin of a citrus fruit, used as a flavouring or garnish.
- **Hindi:** ज़ेस्ट
- **Examples:**
  1. The orange zest gives the strong flavor in this dish.
  2. I add zest to the meat by rubbing it with a spice mixture before grilling.
