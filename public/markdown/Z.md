## 1: Zealous
- **Dissection:** [zeal-ous]
- **Meaning:** Full of zeal; ardent, fervent; exhibiting enthusiasm or strong passion.
- **Hindi:** उत्साही
- **Examples:**
  1. The zealous volunteers worked late into the night to prepare the shelter.
  2. She was a zealous supporter of environmental conservation initiatives.

## 2: Zenith
- **Dissection:** [zenith]
- **Meaning:** The point in the sky vertically above a given position or observer; the point in the celestial sphere opposite the nadir.
- **Hindi:** शीर्षबिंदु
- **Examples:**
  1. Winning the continental championship was the zenith of my career.
  2. The sun reached its zenith in the sky, casting short shadows on the ground.

## 3: Zest
- **Dissection:** [zest]
- **Meaning:** The outer skin of a citrus fruit, used as a flavouring or garnish.
- **Hindi:** उत्तेजकता
- **Examples:**
  1. The orange zest gives the strong flavor in this dish.
  2. I add zest to the meat by rubbing it with a spice mixture before grilling.
